# POWER OUTAGE / ELECTRICITY · Visual Bible

## Continuity anchor

- Modern protagonist: Chinese adult in early thirties, short dark hair, plain off-white T-shirt, thoughtful rather than theatrical.
- Apartment: modest contemporary bedroom/living room, standing fan near a window, wall air-conditioner, router on a shelf, phone on bedside table, neighboring apartment blocks visible outside.
- Scene 1, 2 and 5 must preserve the same protagonist, fan, router shelf, window geometry and camera axis.

## Art direction

- Hand-drawn editorial illustration combining colored pencil, cyanotype technical sketch and restrained risograph misregistration.
- Visible warm paper grain and human linework. No glossy 3D render, neon cyberpunk, photorealism or comic speech balloons.
- Palette: midnight indigo `#101a35`; emergency amber `#e6a85c`; copper orange `#b8663b`; electric cyan `#54c7d9`; dawn paper `#eee6d4`.
- 16:9 cinematic compositions with safe negative space for deterministic title, dates and subtitles.

## Scene locks

1. **Absence** — stopped fan, dark air-conditioner and router, phone flashlight, humid stillness. No supernatural fear.
2. **Dependencies** — same room and person; subtle hand-drawn network echoes reveal refrigerator, water pump, elevator and distant communication tower as systems, not floating UI icons.
3. **Controllable current** — anonymous period hands, a historically plausible voltaic pile and Faraday ring-coil experiment; candle and copper accents; no portrait, label or modern instrument.
4. **From device to system** — anonymous late-nineteenth-century engineers, incandescent lamp endpoint, generator room, distribution conductors and transformer logic readable as a connected panorama; no corporate logo or hero portrait.
5. **Return** — exact modern room relationship from scene 1; fan spins, router lights, soft lamp and city windows return; protagonist relieved but reflective, not celebrating.

## Prohibitions

- No generated text, dates, equations, watermarks or logos inside the images.
- No Edison/Tesla/Faraday celebrity portrait and no claim that one person invented electricity.
- No lightning-bolt spectacle as a substitute for infrastructure.
- No unsafe handling of exposed live conductors.
